FAQ

Pricing questions.

No. Launch and Dominate are month-to-month with no setup cost. Own Your Market carries a 6-month minimum commitment since it includes zip code exclusivity.

No — ad spend on Own Your Market is billed separately and client-funded, typically $1,000/mo minimum, on top of the plan price.

Yes. Most contractors start on Launch or Dominate and move up once the leads are flowing — see the full system for what each tier includes.
