FAQ

Can I use a regular shop vac instead of a purpose-built dust extractor?

A standard shop vac without HEPA filtration will pass fine silica particles straight through and back into the air, largely defeating the point. If budget is the constraint, at minimum look for a shop vac with a certified HEPA filter upgrade, even if it lacks auto-cleaning.

Does the extractor need to match my grinder brand?

No — most extractors connect via a standard or adapter-compatible hose to any shrouded grinder. Brand pairing (like DEWALT-to-DEWALT) is a convenience, not a requirement.

Is a respirator still necessary if I have a good dust extractor?

Yes. Dust collection reduces airborne exposure significantly but doesn't eliminate it — wear a properly rated respirator (P100/organic-vapor cartridges) any time you're grinding, regardless of your extraction setup.

How do I know when the filter needs cleaning or replacing?

A noticeable drop in suction is the main sign — auto-cleaning extractors handle this automatically during use, but the filter itself still needs periodic manual cleaning or replacement per the manufacturer's schedule, especially after a large job.
