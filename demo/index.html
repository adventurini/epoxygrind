Our Process

Four steps to a floor that lasts.

STEP 01

Consultation & Surface Review

We inspect the slab, test for moisture, and check for cracks or prior coatings before recommending a system.

STEP 02

System Recommendation

Flake, metallic, solid, or polyaspartic — matched to your traffic, budget, and how the space gets used.

STEP 03

Prep & Installation

We grind, not acid-etch — mechanical profiling opens the pores of the concrete so the coating actually bonds instead of peeling in a year.

STEP 04

Walkthrough & Care Instructions

A full walkthrough of your new floor plus exactly how to care for it during cure and beyond.

Flake Systems
