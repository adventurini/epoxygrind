Systems We Install

Every system, available in North Sampleville.

Full-Flake Epoxy

Vinyl color chips broadcast to full coverage over a base coat — the most popular residential choice, forgiving of minor slab imperfections and available in dozens of blends.

Metallic Epoxy

Metallic pigments suspended in clear epoxy, manipulated while wet to create a marbled, 3D depth effect — a genuine showroom-floor look for a garage or basement.

Solid Color Epoxy

A clean, single-color satin or gloss finish — the most budget-friendly residential system, still far more durable than paint.
