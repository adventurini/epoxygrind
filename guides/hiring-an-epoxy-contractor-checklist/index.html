Questions to ask before you sign anything

  1. How do you prep the slab? The answer should be mechanical diamond grinding, not acid etching alone — see why this matters.
  2. Do you test for moisture, and what happens if it fails? They should have a real answer beyond "we haven't had an issue."
  3. What product and solids percentage do you use? 100%-solids epoxy or polyaspartic outperforms retail-tier water-based products — see what solids content means.
  4. Are you licensed and insured? Ask to see proof, not just a verbal yes.
  5. What's the warranty, in writing? Get specific coverage and duration — a verbal "we stand behind our work" isn't a warranty.
  6. Can I see photos of similar completed jobs? A contractor with real experience will have these readily available.

Red flags to walk away from

  • A quote with no mention of surface prep or moisture testing at all
  • One-day-completion promises — proper prep, coating, and cure genuinely takes several days
  • No written estimate, or reluctance to put the warranty in writing
  • Pricing dramatically below every other quote you've gotten (a real red flag, not a deal)
  • Pressure to sign same-day without time to compare quotes

FAQ

What questions should I ask an epoxy contractor before hiring?

Ask about their prep method (grinding vs. etching), moisture testing process, product solids content, licensing/insurance, and get the warranty terms in writing.

What are red flags when hiring an epoxy contractor?

No mention of surface prep or moisture testing, one-day-completion promises, no written estimate, unusually low pricing, or pressure to sign immediately.

Should an epoxy contractor be licensed and insured?

Yes — always ask for proof of insurance before work starts; requirements for formal licensing vary by state but insurance protects you regardless.
